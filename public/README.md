# DigiMunshi Landing Page Assets

Place your production assets directly in this `public/` directory:

1. **`demo-video.mp4`**: Your demo screen recording video.
   - When present, the HTML5 video player on the landing page will play it automatically.
   - You can also paste an unlisted YouTube or Vimeo iframe into `index.html` inside `#videoFrame`.
2. **`digimunshi.apk`**: Your production Android APK build.
   - Clicking "ڈاؤن لوڈ کریں (Download APK)" on the landing page triggers a direct download of this file.
3. **`favicon.svg`**: App logo icon (already included).
4. **`og-preview.png`**: WhatsApp & social share preview card.
